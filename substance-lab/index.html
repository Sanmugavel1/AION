Capabilities

What AION makes visible.

Hover or tap a layer to see what it does.

  • Organizational Intelligence Index 01

    One health score from 12 live signals — velocity, coverage, resilience, collaboration and more — the whole organization's cognitive health at a glance.

  • Knowledge Decay & Half-life 02

    Every document has a half-life. AION measures how fast each domain goes stale and surfaces what's being quietly forgotten — before it's gone.

  • Disease Detection 03

    A continuous scan for five organizational pathologies — cancer, Alzheimer's, communication stroke, obesity and cognitive decline — each with a severity score and days-to-critical.

  • Future Simulation 04

    Model a what-if before it's real: simulate a key departure or a department closure and watch the cascade of knowledge, projects and revenue at risk.

  • Self-Healing & Board Advisor 05

    AION doesn't just diagnose — it recommends the fix, routes it to the right owner, and briefs your board in plain language grounded in live data.

  • Executive Command Center 06

    One screen that answers the questions leadership actually asks: which department is losing knowledge fastest, which is most at risk, where training should go. Each answer traces back to the department that produced it.

  • Departments & Approval Chain 07

    Six isolated department workspaces with a real approval chain: employee, then manager, then department head. Isolation is enforced on the server, not hidden in the interface, and every decision leaves an immutable trail.

  • Cross-Department Marketplace 08

    Teams keep their own files private by default. Anything approved can be published for the rest of the company to borrow, rate and reuse, so one team stops solving what another already solved.

  • Risk Prediction & Memory Timeline 09

    Forward-looking warnings that state how likely they are, why, what it costs to ignore them and what to do, alongside a searchable chronological record of everything the organization has learned.

  • Learning Center 10

    When a subject is barely written down, AION points at the documents that do cover it rather than only naming the gap. Effectiveness is measured against what actually changed afterwards, not against course completions.

Every departure is a memory-loss event. AION is the part of your organization that remembers.
